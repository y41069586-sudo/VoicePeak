# Medical Disclaimer

> **DRAFT — have a lawyer review before publishing.** Localized versions

Glowé is a tracking and education tool, not medical advice, and not a
substitute for a dermatologist. All skin readings are estimates, not clinical
measurements. Glowé shows what happened on your skin — it never claims a
product works or diagnoses any condition. If you have a skin concern, please
consult a qualified professional.
