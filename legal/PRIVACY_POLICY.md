## On-device by design
Glowé analyzes your face and skin **entirely on your device**. Your photos are
processed locally and are **never uploaded, transmitted, or collected by us**.

## What we store
On your device only: your skin profile (skin type, concerns, sensitivities,
goal), scan estimates (numbers), your products and routine, and local photo
thumbnails. **We do not sell your data.**

## Optional cloud features
Account sync, community efficacy data, and affiliate features are **off by
default**. If you enable them, only anonymized numeric data — **never photos** —
is processed, and only with your consent.

## Your rights
Export your data or delete everything at any time in **Settings → Data &
privacy**. Under the GDPR you also have rights of access, rectification,
erasure, and portability. Contact: **[email]**.

## Minors
Glowé is not directed to children under 16.

## Product data
Product information comes from the open-licensed **Open Beauty Facts** database.
