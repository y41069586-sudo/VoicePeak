2. Processing of your photos (skin analysis)

The core feature of the app is the analysis of a selfie you take. For this purpose your photo is transmitted in encrypted form to the following processors and used there exclusively to create your analysis:

  • Perfect Corp (YouCam AI) – determines cosmetic skin metrics (e.g. pores, redness, texture).
  • Google (Gemini API) – optionally creates an AI preview of your skin's potential ("10/10" image).

Transmission only happens when you actively start a scan. We do not use your photos for advertising and never sell them to third parties. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1)(b) GDPR (performance of contract) and your consent for special categories of data (Art. 9 (2)(a) GDPR).

What face data we collect: the photograph you take (or select from your library) for a scan — an ordinary image of your face. The app does not create a faceprint, facial-geometry template or any biometric identifier, does not use Face ID or TrueDepth data, and does not use your photo to identify you.

Sharing: your photo is shared only with the two processors named above, only to produce the analysis you requested, and with no one else. It is never sold, never used for advertising, and never used by us to train AI models.

3. Storage, retention and deletion of face data

Your scan photos are stored only locally on your device. We do not operate any server that stores your photos, and photos are never uploaded to our account database. Photos remain on your device until you delete them: "Delete everything" in the settings removes every stored scan photo immediately, and deleting the app removes them as well. The transmission to the processors named in section 2 happens once per scan for the sole purpose of computing your results; we do not retain any copy of the photo outside your device.

4. Account & sync (optional)

If you create an account, we store your email address and your analysis metrics (without photos) with our hosting partner Supabase to back up your data across devices. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1)(b) GDPR.

5. Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled by Apple (App Store). To attribute your purchase status we use RevenueCat, which processes an anonymous user ID — no payment data. Payment details remain entirely with Apple.

6. Notifications

Reminders (e.g. for your routine) are scheduled locally on your device. No external push servers are used.

7. No tracking, no ads

The app contains no ad networks and no cross-device tracking.

8. This website

When you visit this page, our hosting provider (Netlify) processes technically necessary server log data (including IP address and time of access). Legal basis: Art. 6 (1)(f) GDPR.

9. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR), as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. Just email us at the address above.

10. Medical disclaimer

SkinFix is a cosmetic and lifestyle product. The analysis is not a medical diagnosis and does not replace advice from a doctor or dermatologist.
