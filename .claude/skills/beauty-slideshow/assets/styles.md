# Style rotation — index `(idx - 1) % 14`

Each entry gives photography, typography, text density and two palettes.
Palette A on odd `idx`, palette B on even `idx`.

Palettes are listed as `bg · surface · ink · accent`.

---

## 0 · RAW UGC
Looks like a creator's own camera roll. Bathroom, bedroom, mirror. Natural
daylight, slightly off framing, real skin, casual product placement, mild grain.
Text sits directly on the photo, no containers, no decoration.
**Type:** bold sans, white, soft drop shadow. **Density:** low, 1 line per slide.
**A:** `#F3EEE5 · #FFFFFF · #222222 · #F4A6BE`
**B:** `#EFE7DF · #FFFFFF · #2A211C · #C98B6B`
**Best for:** mistakes, "what actually helped", routines, confessions.

## 1 · DARK BEAUTY EDITORIAL
Moody. Dark neutral backdrop, close-up skincare texture, dramatic but realistic
shadow, product half in shadow. Sparse text, lots of black.
**Type:** elegant sans, generous letter-spacing, small. **Density:** very low.
**A:** `#1C1917 · #E7DED4 · #F5F1EC · #F4A6BE`
**B:** `#17161A · #DDD6CE · #FFFFFF · #C9A227`
**Best for:** actives, night routine, "the one thing that worked".

## 2 · PINK CREATOR CAROUSEL
Bright feminine TikTok beauty. Rounded text boxes, bold condensed type, small
handwritten accents, arrows and underlines. Higher information density.
**Type:** bold condensed + a handwritten accent word. **Density:** high.
**A:** `#FFF3F6 · #FFFFFF · #111111 · #F4A6BE`
**B:** `#FDEEF2 · #FFFFFF · #2B1B22 · #E76A8D`
**Best for:** acne mistakes, ingredient lists, beginner guides.

## 3 · MINIMAL PHOTO + BIG STATEMENT
One strong photograph, huge headline, one supporting line, lots of empty space.
Understood in under two seconds while scrolling.
**Type:** very large bold sans. **Density:** minimal, 1 headline per slide.
**A:** `#FFFFFF · #F5F3EF · #111111 · #F4A6BE`
**B:** `#F5F3EF · #FFFFFF · #1C1917 · #6E5548`
**Best for:** single strong claims, myth-busting, before/after framing.

## 4 · NOTES APP
Looks like a screenshot of someone's phone notes. Off-white background, black
text, list format, occasional highlight. Intentionally plain — no photography
on the body slides, one photo on the hook only.
**Type:** plain system sans, left aligned. **Density:** high, list.
**A:** `#FFFFFF · #F7F7F5 · #111111 · #F4A6BE`
**B:** `#FDFBF7 · #FFFFFF · #1A1A1A · #7FA88C`
**Best for:** "things I stopped doing", rules, product lists, honest rants.

## 5 · PHOTO COLLAGE
Two to four photos in one vertical composition, slight overlap, irregular
placement, handwritten annotations between them. Pinterest energy.
**Type:** small sans labels + handwritten accents. **Density:** medium.
**A:** `#F3EEE5 · #FFFFFF · #222222 · #F4A6BE`
**B:** `#EDF1EA · #FFFFFF · #22261F · #7FA88C`
**Best for:** routines, product roundups, a week of progress.

## 6 · PROGRESS
Transformation. Two frames, simple labels, authentic and unretouched. Never
overstate — label with time elapsed, not with a result claim.
**Type:** small plain labels, one bold headline. **Density:** very low.
**A:** `#F5F3EF · #FFFFFF · #1C1917 · #F4A6BE`
**B:** `#EFE7DF · #FFFFFF · #2A211C · #C98B6B`
**Best for:** consistency stories, "3 weeks in", habit changes.
**Careful:** no miracle framing, no "cured". Show a change, not a promise.

## 7 · PRODUCT FLATLAY
Top-down on a bathroom counter, bed or floor. Products arranged naturally,
not styled. Text floats around them with small arrows.
**Type:** clean sans, small, with one bold headline. **Density:** medium.
**A:** `#F3EEE5 · #FFFFFF · #222222 · #C98B6B`
**B:** `#FFF3F6 · #FFFFFF · #111111 · #F4A6BE`
**Best for:** "what's actually in my routine", minimal routines, budget picks.
**Never** show a readable brand name or logo. Labels stay blank.

## 8 · EDUCATIONAL CAROUSEL
Highly scannable. Large number, bold headline, one idea, supporting image.
Strong hierarchy, immediately readable.
**Type:** oversized numeral + bold sans headline. **Density:** medium.
**A:** `#FFFFFF · #F5F3EF · #111111 · #F4A6BE`
**B:** `#DDE5D5 · #FFFFFF · #222222 · #4F6B57`
**Best for:** "3 things", "5 mistakes", "7 rules".

## 9 · SCREENSHOT AESTHETIC
Feels lifted from someone's own content — a comment, a DM, a reply. Casual,
conversational, imperfect. Answer a real question a viewer would ask.
**Type:** plain, slightly imperfect. **Density:** medium.
**A:** `#FFFFFF · #F2F2F5 · #111111 · #F4A6BE`
**B:** `#F7F7F5 · #FFFFFF · #1A1A1A · #5B8DEF`
**Best for:** Q&A, objections, "someone asked me this".
Invent the question; never fake a real person's username or handle.

## 10 · BEAUTY MAGAZINE
Editorial. Photography dominates, subtle overlay, muted colour, minimal text,
serif and sans paired.
**Type:** serif headline + small sans caption. **Density:** low.
**A:** `#EFE7DF · #FFFFFF · #2A211C · #6E5548`
**B:** `#1C1917 · #E7DED4 · #F5F1EC · #C9A227`
**Best for:** ingredients, "the science bit", seasonal skin.

## 11 · RELATABLE / MEME
Conversational and funny. Expressive photo, one large readable line, almost no
design. Slightly chaotic on purpose.
**Type:** big bold sans, that's it. **Density:** one line.
**A:** `#FFFFFF · #F5F3EF · #111111 · #F4A6BE`
**B:** `#FFF3F6 · #FFFFFF · #111111 · #E76A8D`
**Best for:** shared frustrations, skincare fails, "tell me you have oily skin".

## 12 · CLEAN GIRL
Bright, airy, soft. Natural daylight, clean bathroom, cream and white, soft
shadows, delicate typography.
**Type:** light sans, small, generous spacing. **Density:** low.
**A:** `#FFFFFF · #F5F3EF · #222222 · #C98B6B`
**B:** `#F3EEE5 · #FFFFFF · #2A211C · #F4A6BE`
**Best for:** morning routine, minimalism, "doing less".

## 13 · ONE IDEA PER SCREEN
The simplest possible slideshow. One visual, one bold statement, one short
supporting sentence. Nothing else on the slide, ever.
**Type:** one weight, one size, centred. **Density:** minimal.
**A:** `#F5F3EF · #FFFFFF · #1C1917 · #F4A6BE`
**B:** `#1C1917 · #E7DED4 · #FFFFFF · #F4A6BE`
**Best for:** anything that keeps coming out too wordy in another style.
